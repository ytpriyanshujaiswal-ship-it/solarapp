FAQ

Aam sawaal

Mere liye kaunsa solar system sahi hai? +

Yeh aapke bijli connection, daily load, backup ki zaroorat aur budget par depend karta hai. Stable grid wale zyadatar ghar ke liye on-grid theek rehta hai; backup chahiye to off-grid ya hybrid. Site survey ke baad sahi salah milti hai.

Chhat par kitni jagah chahiye? +

Moti-moti taur par 1 kW solar panels ke liye lagbhag 80–100 sq ft shadow-free chhat chahiye. Exact jagah panels ke type par depend karti hai.

Kya subsidy milti hai? +

PM Surya Ghar jaisi government schemes aapki category aur current rules ke anusaar lagu ho sakti hain. Hum eligibility aur documents mein guide karte hain; final eligibility authorities tay karti hain.

Installation mein kitna time lagta hai? +

Time approvals, equipment availability aur system size par depend karta hai. Quotation ke saath hum expected schedule bata denge.

Kya site survey hota hai? +

Ji haan. Apni location ke saath call ya WhatsApp karein, hum site visit arrange karenge, uske baad final quotation denge.

Apne ghar par solar lagwana hai?

Apna monthly bill bhejein, hum aapko suitable system ke options samjhayenge.
